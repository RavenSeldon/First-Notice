- **Authorship:** Separate AI-generated concepts, code, or drafts from participant decisions and final human-authored policy arguments.
- **Corrections:** Append new dated records; correct earlier statements transparently rather than silently erasing their history.
- **Privacy:** Do not enter access tokens, credentials, or confidential source material.
