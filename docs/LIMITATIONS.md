# Limitations

- The registry, routing, recipients and clocks are simulated. The 48 h notice clock and 24 h acknowledgment clock are proposals, not law.
- Existing Canadian rules (C15, C16) already require GC departments to report to the Cyber Centre. This protocol only proposes the external developer → government leg.
- Procurement leverage does not reach unrelated private research or unknown operators of open-weight models.
- Mandatory reporting may chill disclosure if confidentiality, liability and competitive consequences are badly designed (C10, C13).
- C07 and C09 rest on the developer's own account only.
- The counterfactual uses 15 Aug 2026 as a stand-in for "mid-August" (C02).
- Sources partially read: the GC CSEMP (~52%) and the ISED consultation (~80%).
